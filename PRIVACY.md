## 2. Data we collect

**Account and contact data**

- Email address and authentication metadata if you sign in or create an account.
- Support messages and related contact details if you contact us.

**Agent activity and uploaded content**

- AI coding-agent transcripts, messages, prompts, outputs, command summaries, recommendation inputs, and related metadata you submit, upload, connect, or cause the Service to collect.
- Project identifiers, repository metadata, tool names, agent names, session identifiers, timestamps, and workflow context needed to generate recommendations.
- Generated recommendations, summaries, skills, checks, prompts, and workflow suggestions.

Together, these materials are **"User Content"**.

**Usage, token, and operational data**

- Request counts, processed-token counts, recommendation counts, timestamps, dashboard activity, quota/rate-limit state, script status, delivery state, retry metadata, and basic performance/error metrics.
- API token issuance, revocation, expiry, status, and access logs where token-based access is used.

**Network, security, and abuse-prevention data**

- IP addresses, user-agent strings, device/browser metadata, timestamps, logs, edge-security events, fraud-prevention signals, abuse-prevention signals, and other technical metadata needed to deliver, protect, debug, and secure the Service.

We do not intentionally collect special category data. Please do not submit secrets, credentials, regulated data, sensitive personal data, or confidential third-party information unless you have the right to do so and understand the risks.

We do **not** sell personal data.

We process User Content to provide, operate, secure, troubleshoot, support, and improve the Service, including generating recommendations, maintaining reliability, detecting abuse, debugging failures, and improving product behaviour.

Where the Service uses third-party AI model providers or infrastructure providers, those providers may process requests under their own terms, privacy policies, data-processing terms, retention settings, and abuse-monitoring rules. We aim to use providers and configurations appropriate for the Service, but we do not control every third-party provider policy.

## 7. Retention

We retain personal data only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including operating the Service, generating and displaying recommendations, maintaining account access, securing the Service, resolving disputes, enforcing terms, complying with law, and maintaining reasonable backups.

Typical retention approach:

- Account and contact data: retained while your account exists or while needed for support, legal, security, or operational reasons.
- Agent activity, transcripts, uploads, recommendations, and usage data: retained while needed to provide recommendations, dashboard history, debugging, security, abuse prevention, or product operation.
- Security, rate-limit, fraud-prevention, and abuse-prevention logs: retained as needed to protect the Service and investigate misuse.
- Deletion requests: upon verified request or account deletion, we aim to delete or anonymise personal data within 30 days, with residual backups purged within up to 90 days where technically and legally practical.

We may retain limited records longer where required or permitted by law, to resolve disputes, enforce terms, prevent abuse, or protect the Service.
